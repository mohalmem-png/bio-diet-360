🧬 The Science of Food

🍞 Carbohydrates

Building blocks: monosaccharides.

Structure → function: glucose supports ATP production; glycosidic linkages connect sugars in starch and fiber.

🥚 Proteins

Building blocks: amino acids joined by peptide bonds.

Structure → function: amino acid sequence influences folding, enzymes and tissue repair.

🥑 Lipids

Components: many fats contain glycerol and fatty acids.

Structure → function: hydrophobic chains aid energy storage; phospholipids form cell membranes.

🧬 Nucleic Acids

Building blocks: nucleotides.

Structure → function: DNA stores genetic information; RNA participates in protein production. They are not major dietary energy sources.
